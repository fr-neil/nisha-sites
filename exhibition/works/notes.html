  1. 1What an entry is

    An entry is a row of the research record (111 rows, closed Sep 2026): a hosting credit she published herself (a caption, a story, her own poster or LinkedIn), from her first full-time show on 9 Sep 2016. Guest or creator appearances, private occasions and anything earlier are excluded.

  2. 2This is not a complete list

    The catalogue names 65 clients. Her posts name about 120 organisations, 2015–2026, including some before 9 Sep 2016 and some without a dated hosting entry. Some events were never posted.

  3. 3Codes

    NS- + the record serial. Codes are stable, never reused and not in date order.

  4. 4Addresses

    Every code has one permanent address:

    • documented entries: works/ns-104.html
    • all others: works/index.html?code=NS-050#ns-050
    • plates: works/plates.html#pl-17
    • clips: watch/index.html#ns-111a

  5. 5Dates

    Jun 2026
    The month and year she gives.
    2025
    Year only. These rows sort at the end of their year.
    (uncertain)
    Her posts give different dates; the date shown is from her own post.
    Date uncertain
    Her posts give different years. The entry sorts last.

  6. 6Places

    City, Country
    Or the country alone.
    Cruise leg क्रूज़ पड़ाव
    A country seen only as a cruise departure or route (Singapore, Hong Kong SAR). Named, not counted.
    Not stated उल्लेख नहीं
    She gives no country (NS-089).

  7. 7Symbols

    —
    Not stated in her post.
    Italics
    Her own anonymising words, never a guess.
    Client not named
    She did not name the client.
    Client withheld क्लाइंट का नाम रोका गया
    She named the client; the event was booked through an event agency, so the client is not shown.
    Again दोबारा
    She says she had hosted for this client before. Never counted.
    ●Filled dot
    Documented in this edition: plates or clips (12).
    ▶Play mark
    A clip with her voice on 04 Watch (3 entries, 4 clips).

  8. 8Industry

    Industry is our classification, not hers.

  9. 9Language

    Language is filled only where her voice is heard in a clip, or where her post states it.

  10. 10How we counted

    • 111 = the hosting credits she published, 9 Sep 2016 to Sep 2026.
    • 65 = distinct client names among the 84 entries that name one. Joint names such as “EY & Microsoft” count once, and HDFC counts once. 61 are listed in 02.03 and 4 are withheld (item 7).
    • 32 entries outside India. 16 places = 15 countries and Macao SAR. Singapore and Hong Kong SAR appear only as cruise legs (NS-020, NS-027), so they are named but not counted.
    • 13 = clients with two or more entries. HDFC is left out because its two entries may be different entities.
    • 10 formats = our classification.
    • Languages: English and Hindi, from her posts.
    • Counted from her public posts, to Sep 2026.

  11. 11Chronology rule

    A line appears in 01.02 only if it is one of:

    • a career fact from LinkedIn or her captions;
    • a first she named;
    • the first entry in a format;
    • the first entry in a country outside India;
    • a documented entry (●documented).

    One entry per line. Entries whose year is uncertain are not placed in the chronology.

  12. 12Sources

    Each entry links to her original post where one exists. Stories have no public link.

  13. 13Plates and clips

    • Plates are numbered Pl. 01–19 by the date of the photograph, or of her post where the photograph is undated (Pl. 09); Pl. 19 comes last because its date is uncertain.
    • Outside the catalogue कार्य-सूची से बाहर: a hosting post found after the record was closed, or not yet confirmed as an entry. It is captioned from her caption and never counted (6 plates).
    • Photographs are printed in black and white. Frames taken from video are printed as a halftone screen. Clips play in colour when you press play.
    • Clips with her voice start at a pause before she begins and end after she finishes the sentence.
    • Captions are timed to her speech. Lines 1–4 of NS-111a are checked against her own post and may be quoted.

  14. 14No results

    No entries match कोई प्रविष्टि नहीं मिली and Clear filters फ़िल्टर हटाएँ. Values with zero results are disabled.

  15. 15Edition

    Edition 01, October 2026. Data from her public posts to Sep 2026.
