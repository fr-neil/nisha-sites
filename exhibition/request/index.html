Requestअनुरोध

Request availability in three steps. You enter your event, check it, then send it to her yourself. This site does not store or send your details.

06What to includeक्या शामिल करें

  1. Enquiring as आप कौन हैं: the company hosting the event, an event agency, or other
  2. Format प्रारूप
  3. Date or month तारीख़ या महीना, and whether dates are flexible
  4. City and country शहर और देश
  5. In person or virtual प्रत्यक्ष (ऑफ़लाइन) या वर्चुअल
  6. Hosting language होस्टिंग की भाषा
  7. Audience size दर्शकों की संख्या
  8. Organisation संस्था
  9. Your name आपका नाम
  10. Reply to जवाब कहाँ दें: your phone, WhatsApp or email

Write these in a message to @nishashetty22 on Instagram.
