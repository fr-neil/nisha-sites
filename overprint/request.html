Request availability

उपलब्धता पूछें

Enter the details, check them, then send them to her on Instagram.

  1. 01 Enter भरें
  2. 02 Check जाँचें
  3. 03 Send भेजें

What to include क्या लिखें

  • When (a month or a date), and whether the date can move
  • Where (city and country)
  • Format
  • In person or virtual
  • Hosting language
  • Audience size
  • Who is enquiring: the company hosting the event, an event agency, or other
  • Organisation
  • Your name
  • Where she should reply: your phone, WhatsApp or email

Message her on Instagram इंस्टाग्राम पर संदेश भेजें

Send the details in a message to @nishashetty22 on Instagram. This site does not send or store anything.
