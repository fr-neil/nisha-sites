What is counted क्या गिना गया
Hosting credits she published herself (captions, stories, her own posters, LinkedIn), from her first full-time show on 9 September 2016 to September 2026: 111 events. Guest appearances and private occasions are not included. Some events were never posted, so this is not a complete list of her work.
How the numbers are counted गिनती कैसे हुई
111 events: 78 in India; 32 outside India, in 15 countries and Macao SAR and on two cruises that left from Singapore and Hong Kong (those ports are named, not counted as countries); 1 at sea, with no country stated. Places: 36 named cities (16 in India, 20 outside India) and 3 voyages. An event in two cities is shown in both and counted once. 12 events in India name no city; 11 virtual or on-camera events name no venue. Clients: 65 named in her posts; 61 are named here, and 4, booked through event agencies, are named once the agencies agree. 12 virtual or on-camera events; 6 booked through event agencies. Formats are our classification.
Came back दोबारा साथ
A client with two or more events here, or one whose event she describes as a return, in her own words. HDFC is left out because its two events may be different entities. No numbers of returns are given.
Dates तारीख़ें
Month and year from her post. Where her posts give only the year, only the year is printed. One event’s date is not settled in her posts, so no date is printed for it.
Captions कैप्शन
The first line gives client, event, venue and date from her post. Where a picture comes from a post about an event outside the 111, the first line gives only what that post, its caption or its video title says; Pictures by page names the source. The second line describes what the picture shows. Pictures are printed in two inks; the clips play in full colour.
Her words उनके शब्द
The words on p. 16 are from her reel of the opening of an awards night, checked against the words shown in that reel. Other speech is heard in the clips, with automatic captions.
Clips क्लिप
Each clip starts before she speaks, or at the start of her reel, and ends after she finishes a thought, or at the natural end of the reel. Sound plays only when you tap. The silent picture on p. 6 plays once.
