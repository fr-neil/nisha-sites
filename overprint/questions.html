Q. 01

Q. 01. Where did it start? शुरुआत कहाँ से हुई?

  1. Dec 2013 – Sep 2016Senior Software Engineer, IGATE.
  2. From Apr 2015Her Instagram shows her hosting, while she still works in IT.
  3. 9 Sep 2016Her first show as a full-time emcee, the Audi A4 launch.

Q. 02

Q. 02. Which languages does she host in? वे किन भाषाओं में होस्ट करती हैं?

English and Hindi.

→ On the mic, p. 16

Q. 03

Q. 03. What kinds of events? किस तरह के कार्यक्रम?

Awards nights and R&R evenings most often in this issue (26 of 111), then corporate events (17), conferences and summits (16), launches (13), dealer and partner meets (12), virtual and on-camera work (12), celebrity and press events (7), institutional and cultural events (4), pageants (2) and red carpets (2).

→ Formats, p. 20→ Index, p. 29

Q. 04

Q. 04. Does she host outside India? क्या वे भारत के बाहर होस्ट करती हैं?

Yes: 32 of the 111 events, in 15 countries and Macao SAR, and on two cruises that left from Singapore and Hong Kong. The first was an HDFC gala on the Chao Phraya, Bangkok, in January 2017. A third cruise, its route not stated, is counted on its own, at sea.

→ Cape Town, p. 21→ Places, p. 22→ Index, p. 29

Q. 05

Q. 05. Do clients ask her back? क्या क्लाइंट उन्हें दोबारा बुलाते हैं?

Yes. In this issue: HSBC, 2023 to 2026; Raychem RPG, 2025 and 2026; Dormer Pramet, in Colombo in December 2025 and April 2026; Himalaya, twice in Jaipur in 2025; TATA AIA Life, 2024 and 2025.

→ People and clients, p. 18→ Index, p. 29

Q. 06

Q. 06. How does she prepare? वे तैयारी कैसे करती हैं?

Her posts don’t describe it. What they show: script cards in her hand on pp. 8, 12 and 13 of this issue, a programme booklet on p. 15, and a July 2025 post from Tbilisi that lists events, meetings and scripting.

→ p. 8→ Cards in hand, pp. 12–15

Q. 07

Q. 07. Does she host virtual and on-camera events? क्या वे वर्चुअल और कैमरे पर होस्ट करती हैं?

Yes: 12 events in this issue, 2020 to 2025, from virtual awards to a filmed series for NSE India.

Q. 08

Q. 08. Does she host panels and interviews? क्या वे पैनल और इंटरव्यू होस्ट करती हैं?

Yes: the WOW Dialogues panels for EVENTFAQS in New Delhi, July 2019; a studio show for Pinkvilla, “Let’s Talk Fashion”, December 2020; and an on-camera interview for Pinkvilla × Lionsgate Play, January 2022.

→ WOW Dialogues, p. 15

p. 27

Q. 09

Q. 09. Does she host launches? क्या वे लॉन्च होस्ट करती हैं?

Yes: 13 launches in this issue, 2016 to 2026, from the Audi A4 in September 2016 to Standard Chartered’s SC WIN in May 2026. Three are pictured: Phoenix Palladium’s new brands, Mumbai, December 2024; OTM India’s Taiwan Pavilion opening, Mumbai, February 2024; and Shapoorji Pallonji’s SP Kingstown, Pune, December 2023.

→ Phoenix Palladium, p. 13→ OTM India, p. 5→ SP Kingstown, p. 6

Q. 10

Q. 10. Does she host institutional and cultural events? क्या वे संस्थागत और सांस्कृतिक कार्यक्रम होस्ट करती हैं?

Yes: 4 institutional and cultural events in this issue, 2017 to 2023, among them the G20 / C20 Food & LiFE Conference in Palghar, May 2023, and the Consolidators Association of India’s 14th anniversary, Mumbai, June 2017.

Q. 11

Q. 11. Does she work with event agencies? क्या वे इवेंट एजेंसियों के साथ काम करती हैं?

Yes. Six events in this issue were booked through event agencies. Agencies can say so on the reply card.

→ Back page, the reply card

Q. 12

Q. 12. How do I ask for a date? तारीख़ के लिए कैसे पूछें?

Fill in the reply card on the back page: enter, check, send. It prepares a message that you send her on Instagram. This site does not send or store anything.
