08How a request works अनुरोध कैसे होता है

  1. 01Enter भरेंYou give your event: when, where, format.
  2. 02Check जाँचेंYou read the details back before anything leaves the page.
  3. 03Send भेजेंThe page prepares a message; you send it to her on Instagram.

Nothing is sent or stored by this site; any reply comes to the contact you gave. Fees, durations and reply times are not shown here.
