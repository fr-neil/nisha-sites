D · Reading rulesEdition 01

Reading rules पढ़ने के नियम

What an event is
A hosting credit she published herself: her captions, stories, her own posters, LinkedIn and her YouTube uploads. Guest appearances and private occasions are not included. It is not a complete list of her work; some events were never posted.
Volumes and lines
A volume is a calendar year. Lines are numbered within the year: dated lines in date order, 01 the earliest, then the lines whose month is not given or not settled. Pages show the newest month first. Lines without a month close their volume, after January, under Month not given; on the rulers they are the dotted tick at the right end of the year. Numbers are never reused or changed.
Dates
May (uncertain): her posts give different months; the month shown is from her own post. Lines before 9 Sep 2016 are lettered and sit in the years before full time.
Clients
Italics: her own anonymising words, never a guess. Where she names no client, the event is shown instead. Client withheld: she named the client, the event was booked through an event agency, and the client is shown once the agency agrees. Againदोबारा: the same client (and, where stated, the same event) had booked her before, by her own words or by dated lines. A marker only; never counted.
Places
Codes are this site’s own, not airport codes: the ISO country code and the first three letters of the city as written in her posts, with these exceptions: IN·DEL New Delhi, IN·SGO South Goa, IN·NAV Navi Mumbai, VN·HCM Ho Chi Minh City, MO·MAC Macao SAR. Positions are approximate (city centres). No borders or coastlines are drawn.
Figures
From her own posts and uploads, shown in black and white; a clip turns to colour while it plays. Clips with her voice start in a pause before she begins and end after she finishes a thought. Not a ledger line: from a post whose event is not in the ledger, captioned from her caption.
